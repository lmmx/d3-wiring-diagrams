Wiring diagrams

Write Python and see its wiring diagram. Each function is a star: its parameters and return are the wires. The operations and calls in its body are the stars inside, and values are the cables between them. How code maps to diagrams

The diagram follows your edits once Python has loaded. Hover a star to find its code; put the cursor in the code to find its stars. Esc then Tab leaves the editor.

An example, a term or a bare diagram, in the shared JSON format. The diagram redraws as you type. Output of term.to_json() (Python) or serde_json::to_string(&term) (Rust) pastes straight in.

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Hover a cable to see everything the composition glues to it. Click a star to see its relation.
